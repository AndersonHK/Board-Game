Card ID: encounter-soot-stall-trader
Display Name: Soot-Stall Trader
Deck: Encounter Deck
Card Type: Encounter
Encounter Class: Random
Scene Tags: social, trader, greed, setup
Escalation Use: Random encounter
Reveal Text: A lantern winks under a cracked arch; rope, oil, knives, and half-truths gleam in the soot-stall. Reveal the top 5 resource cards as trader stock.
Scene Rule: Each player may trade once: direct trade or Haggle.
Clear Condition: All players traded or passed.
Reward Text: None.
Failure Text: None.
Rules Text: Haggle 1d6: 1-2 discard 1 then trade 1-for-1; 3-4 trade 1-for-1; 5-6 trade 1 for 2. Discard unused stock at scene end.
Flavor Text: Certainty is cheaper underground, especially when it is fake.
Art Brief: A soot-streaked merchant stall is wedged into cracked dungeon masonry under a single hanging lantern. Piles of scavenged rope, oil, blades, charms, and dubious maps crowd the counter; the trader should look useful, amused, and impossible to fully trust.
