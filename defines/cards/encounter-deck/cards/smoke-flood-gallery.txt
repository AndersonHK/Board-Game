Card ID: encounter-smoke-flood-gallery
Display Name: Smoke-Flood Gallery
Deck: Encounter Deck
Card Type: Encounter
Encounter Class: Random
Scene Tags: hazard, discard, pressure
Escalation Use: Random encounter
Reveal Text: The old murals vanish under smoke thick enough to bruise and whisper. Each player either discards 1 card or makes the party take 1 damage.
Scene Rule: Spend 1 action to vent with a non-combat test.
Clear Condition: The gallery is vented.
Reward Text: If cleared this round, return 1 card from the discard pile to hand.
Failure Text: If unresolved at round end, the party takes 1 damage.
Rules Text: None.
Flavor Text: The old heroes on the walls disappear first.
Art Brief: A long gallery of statues and heroic murals is swallowed by dark furnace smoke. Make the smoke thick enough to feel physical, bruised purple-black at the edges, with half-seen faces in the murals vanishing as the party pushes through.
