Card ID: encounter-shrine-of-echoes
Display Name: Shrine of Echoes
Deck: Encounter Deck
Card Type: Encounter
Encounter Class: Random
Scene Tags: temptation, reward, enchantment
Escalation Use: Random encounter
Reveal Text: The shrine gives exactly what you ask for and keeps a little of your voice. Each player may draw 1, then discard 1; if 2 or more do, spawn Ash Miasma.
Scene Rule: Players may still take normal actions.
Clear Condition: Bargain resolved.
Reward Text: Resolver gains 1 extra draw.
Failure Text: If Ash Miasma remains at scene end, carry it forward. This overrides normal clearing.
Rules Text: None.
Flavor Text: The shrine answers in your voice, but not with your mercy.
Art Brief: A cracked prayer alcove crowded with ember candles, smoke-script, and tiny reflected mouths in the stone. The image should suggest a bargain happening silently: hands reaching toward light, shadows reaching back, and ash gathering where words should be.
