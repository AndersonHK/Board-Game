Card ID: encounter-shrine-of-echoes
Display Name: Shrine of Echoes
Deck: Encounter Deck
Card Type: Encounter
Encounter Class: Random
Scene Tags: temptation, reward, enchantment
Escalation Use: Random encounter
Reveal Text: Each player may take the shrine bargain once. A player who takes it draws 1 card, then discards 1 card.
Spawn Text: If 2 or more players take the bargain, spawn Ash Miasma.
Scene Rule: Players may still use normal actions while the shrine bargain is active.
Clear Condition: The bargain has been resolved and no blocking card remains.
Reward Text: The player who resolves the shrine gains 1 extra draw.
Failure Text: If Ash Miasma is spawned and remains at end of scene, it stays in play for the next scene.
Treasure Value: 0
Rules Text: This scene creates a selfish setup choice that can carry pressure into later scenes.
Flavor Text: The shrine gives exactly what you ask for, and always keeps a little of your voice.
Art Brief: A cracked prayer alcove full of ember candles and whispering smoke.
