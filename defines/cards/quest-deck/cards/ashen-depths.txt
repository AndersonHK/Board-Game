Setup Text: Start at escalation 0 with a prep scene instead of a hostile battle.

Threshold 10: After the current scene ends, reveal Dragon in the Deep. At escalation 10, ignore the two-turn round cap.
Lose Condition: If party HP reaches 0, all players lose.
Win Condition: Survive the final ordeal and defeat Heartfire Dragon.
Treasure Value: 0
Rules Text: Use the random encounter deck, scripted escalation 5 and 10 ordeals, and a red d20 for party HP. 3P starts at 18 party HP and 4 cards each.
Flavor Text: The ruin keeps breathing long after its builders are ash.
Art Brief: A massive stair descending into a volcanic dungeon, with soot in the air, cracked stone saints, and orange furnace light below.
