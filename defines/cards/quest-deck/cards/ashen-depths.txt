Card ID: quest-ashen-depths
Display Name: Ashen Depths
Deck: Quest Deck
Card Type: Quest
Theme: Classic fantasy dungeon descent through ash-choked ruins
Player Count: 3-4
Starting Party HP (4P): 11
Starting Party HP (3P): 18
Starting Hand Size (4P): 3
Starting Hand Size (3P): 4
Escalation Track: 0-10
Setup Text: Begin at escalation 0 with a prep scene instead of a hostile battle.
Ongoing Quest Rule: When a new scene is fully cleared, raise escalation by 1. If hostile or blocking hurdles remain at end of round, raise escalation by 1.
Threshold 5: After the current scene ends, resolve the scripted ordeal Furnace Warden Rises. Starting with the next scene, the first player each scene to gain extra reward draws must discard 1 card after that reward resolves.
Threshold 8: Ash Pressure Rises. In hostile scenes, the first hostile creature attack each scene gets +1 Attack.
Threshold 10: After the current scene ends, reveal Dragon in the Deep. At escalation 10 the normal two-turn round cap no longer applies.
Lose Condition: If party HP reaches 0, all players lose.
Win Condition: Survive the final ordeal and defeat Heartfire Dragon.
Treasure Value: 0
Rules Text: This quest uses one prep scene, a shuffled random encounter deck, one scripted mid-game ordeal, and one scripted final ordeal. Use a red d20 for party HP. In 3-player mode, start at 18 party HP and 4 cards each.
Flavor Text: The ruin keeps breathing long after its builders are ash.
Art Brief: A massive stair descending into a volcanic dungeon, with soot in the air, cracked stone saints, and orange furnace light below.
