After the simulated playtest is complete, read those non-printed materials and compare them against the playtest logs. The comparison should answer: what did the printed materials teach well, what did they fail to teach, and what knowledge existed only outside the player-facing package?

## Write Scope For Printed-Materials Playtests

During the playtest, write only inside the new session folder under [../temp/playtests/](../temp/playtests/). Do not edit canonical docs, card files, asset docs, scripts, templates, or design files unless explicitly instructed after the findings are reviewed.
