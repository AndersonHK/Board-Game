Draw Philosophy Used In The Simulation
- Baseline end-of-round draws come from the template catalog in fictionally plausible ways.
- Extra draws from card effects are also drawn from this same template catalog.
- The run tracks hand pressure and useful card volume, not final print-sheet frequency.
