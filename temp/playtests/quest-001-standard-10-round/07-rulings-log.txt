Rulings Log For Quest 001

RUL-01 - Prototype deck interpretation
Issue:
- The design docs define a resource-action deck concept, but not whether the 20 listed cards are a final finite deck or a reusable prototype catalog.
Patch used:
- Treat 04-resource-pool.txt as a catalog of repeatable template cards for this run.
Applied:
- Before ROUND-01.
Impact on the run:
- Let the simulation test card economy and role behavior without pretending the deck frequency is already solved.

RUL-02 - Provisional point currency
Issue:
- The docs describe hidden agendas and private scoring pressure, but do not define a general points track.
Patch used:
- Each cleared encounter awards 1 Glory to one claimant among the material contributors.
- The final boss awards 2 Glory.
- Hidden agendas are worth 3 bonus points if completed.
Applied:
- Before ROUND-01.
Impact on the run:
- Made the semi-cooperative tension legible, especially for Kael and Brin.

RUL-03 - Threshold timing
Issue:
- The docs say tension rises after the round, but threshold timing is not yet formalized.
Patch used:
- Threshold effects trigger when tension is raised at end of round and apply to later rounds, not the current round.
Applied:
- Confirmed when ROUND-05 ended and the run moved into ROUND-06.
Impact on the run:
- Cleanly turned on the midpoint escalation without retroactively changing ROUND-05.

RUL-04 - Non-combat resolution language
Issue:
- Merchant, rescue, and vault encounters are not cleanly "combat," but the current system still wants one generic resolution number.
Patch used:
- Any card or ability that directly changes the group problem counts as contribution, even if the fiction is social, navigational, or logistical instead of offensive.
Applied:
- First needed in ROUND-03.
Impact on the run:
- Kept one shared resolution language across all encounter types.

RUL-05 - Damage prevention timing
Issue:
- It was unclear whether prevention changes encounter success or only HP loss.
Patch used:
- First compute total impact, then apply the d6 modifier, then compare to threat.
- If there is a shortfall, prevention and Wards reduce only the HP loss; they do not turn the encounter into a clear.
Applied:
- First needed in ROUND-04.
Impact on the run:
- Produced cleaner consequences and made defensive play about mitigation rather than stealth success conversion.

RUL-06 - Overextend uses party HP, not personal HP
Issue:
- The aggressive active references failure pain, but the run intentionally has no per-player HP track.
Patch used:
- On a failed overextend, add 1 extra damage to the shared party HP instead of tracking personal injury.
Applied:
- First needed in ROUND-06.
Impact on the run:
- Preserved the risk of aggression while keeping the health model simple.

RUL-07 - High-tension agenda band
Issue:
- The Opportunist agenda needed a concrete threshold.
Patch used:
- "High tension" means tension 7 or higher for this run.
Applied:
- Confirmed in ROUND-07.
Impact on the run:
- Made Brin's agenda readable and aligned it with the crisis band described in round-structure.md.

RUL-08 - No hand limit
Issue:
- Hand cap is undefined in the docs.
Patch used:
- No hand limit for this pass.
Applied:
- Implicit from ROUND-01 through ROUND-10.
Impact on the run:
- Mira's hoarding engine snowballed hard and exposed how much draw can accumulate when nothing trims hands.

RUL-09 - Boss persistence intentionally omitted
Issue:
- The docs do not yet define monster HP, phases, or multi-round boss structure.
Patch used:
- Dragon in the Deep is resolved as a single threat 11 encounter with no separate HP track.
Applied:
- ROUND-10.
Impact on the run:
- Good for checking raw number pressure, weak for climax feel.
