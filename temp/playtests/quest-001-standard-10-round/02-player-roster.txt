Simulation Roster: Four Imaginary Friends

1. Kael Stonehand
- Archetype: Aggressive
- Table vibe: loud, competitive, loves landing the killing blow
- Passive: your first committed card each round gets +1 impact if you are first to act
- Active: once per round, Overextend for +2 impact; if the party still fails, add +1 extra party damage

2. Brin Ashvale
- Archetype: Aggressive
- Table vibe: cocky looter, always eyeing the best reward line
- Passive: your first committed card each round gets +1 impact if you are first to act
- Active: once per round, Overextend for +2 impact; if the party still fails, add +1 extra party damage

3. Mira Fen
- Archetype: Defensive
- Table vibe: quiet planner, keeps backup plans and hates losing value
- Passive: if you end the round with 2 or more cards in hand, gain 1 Ward, max 3
- Ward rule for this run: each Ward prevents 1 party damage later
- Active: once per round, spend 1 Ward or discard 1 card to prevent 2 damage

4. Tamsin Vale
- Archetype: Support
- Table vibe: negotiator, stabilizer, tries to keep everyone pointed the same direction
- Passive: when your card directly boosts another player or prevents or heals damage, draw 1
- Active: once per round, give another player +1 impact; if the encounter clears, both players draw 1
