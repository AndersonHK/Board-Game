Encounter Sequence For Quest 001

1. Goblin Sentries - Threat 3
- Two torch-bearing scouts on a broken stair.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory.

2. Collapsed Entry - Threat 4
- The descent caves in and the party must cut a path through stone and smoke.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory.

3. Wandering Merchant - Threat 4
- A soot-covered trader offers shortcuts, rumors, and suspiciously useful supplies.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory and may draw 1.

4. Cursed Idol - Threat 5
- A shrine leaks heat and spite; disturbing it awakens a backlash.
- If failed: the party takes shortfall damage after prevention.

5. Ash Drake Scout - Threat 6
- A juvenile drake circles overhead and calls deeper things awake.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory.

6. Falling Causeway - Threat 6
- Cracked stone spans a lava vent. Every hesitation makes the bridge worse.
- If failed: narrate the loss as attrition, panic, or dropped gear.

7. Captive Pilgrim - Threat 7
- A wounded traveler is chained beside a rune brazier while cultists close in.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory and the party heals 1.

8. Fire Cult Ambush - Threat 7
- Fanatics rush from side tunnels with oil, hooks, and ash masks.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory.

9. Molten Vault - Threat 8
- A treasure chamber opens under pressure seals and unstable magma locks.
- If cleared: claimant gains 1 Glory and may draw 1.

10. Dragon in the Deep - Threat 10
- The chained dragon tears free enough to flood the hall with flame and wingbeats.
- If cleared in this simulation: claimant gains 2 Glory.
