Quest: Ashen Depths
Theme: Classic fantasy dungeon descent

Premise:
The party enters a volcanic ruin beneath a dead mountain, chasing rumors of a chained dragon and a vault full of relics. The deeper they go, the more the ruin wakes up around them.

Core Track:
- Start tension: 0
- Midpoint threshold at tension 5: "The dragon stirs"
- Climax: round 10 final ordeal, Dragon in the Deep

Quest Trait:
- Once per round, one player may Scout: draw 1 card, then discard 1 card.

Threshold Effect:
- The dragon stirs: after tension reaches 5, all later encounters gain +1 threat.

Lose Condition:
- If party HP reaches 0, the entire party loses.

Win Condition For This Simulation:
- Survive all 10 rounds and clear or outlast the final ordeal.

Quest Tone Notes:
- Early rounds should feel like pressure mixed with opportunity.
- Midpoint should feel like the dungeon becoming active, not just numbers going up.
- The boss should expose whether a single-round threat check is enough for climactic monsters.
