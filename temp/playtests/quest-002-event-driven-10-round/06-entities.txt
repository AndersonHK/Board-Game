Entity Cards Used In This Simulation

Enemy entities
- Ash Imp: HP 2, Attack 1. If it survives the scene, it scorches 1 party HP.
- Goblin Skulker: HP 2, Attack 1. On hit, the claimant of the scene loses 1 card.
- Orc Raider: HP 3, Attack 2. On hit, one player discards 1.
- Ogre Brute: HP 5, Attack 3. If it survives a scene, tension +1.
- Brood Wyrm: HP 4, Attack 2. On reveal, tension +1.
- Chainbound Head: HP 5, Attack 2. Boss stage 1.
- Heartfire Dragon: HP 6, Attack 3. Boss stage 2. On reveal, each player either discards 1 or the party takes 1.

Persistent scene cards
- Clinging Miasma: Durability 2. At end of each scene, the player with the most cards discards 1 unless someone spends a magical or support action to dispel it.
- Heavy Door: Durability 4. While closed, the scene's reward cannot be claimed.

Allied entities
- Cinder Familiar: HP 1. Exhaust for +1 on an arcane action or scout.
- Tin Golem: HP 3. Exhaust to prevent 1 damage to party HP.
- Lantern Sprite: HP 1. Exhaust so another player draws 1, then discards 1.
- Grave Hound: HP 2. Exhaust for +1 damage if attacking a target another player also attacked.
