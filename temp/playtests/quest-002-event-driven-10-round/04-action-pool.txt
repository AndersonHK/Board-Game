Action / Resource Pool: Event-Driven Prototype Catalog

Important note:
This is still a template catalog, not a one-copy finalized deck list.

Setup and engine cards
1. Sharpen Edge - Ongoing. Your next damaging action gets +1 damage.
2. Treasure Nose - Ongoing. The first time you claim a reward, draw 2.
3. Map the Tunnels - Ongoing. Later, reroll one save.
4. Pocket the Spoils - If a reward is claimed this scene, you gain 1 extra draw.
5. Hidden Stash - Draw 2.
6. Study the Signs - Peek ahead or gain +1 to your next non-combat test.

Summons and allies
7. Cinder Familiar - Ally. 1 HP. Exhaust: +1 to a magical action or scout effect.
8. Tin Golem - Ally. 3 HP. Exhaust: prevent 1 damage to the party.
9. Lantern Sprite - Ally. 1 HP. Exhaust: another player draws 1, then discards 1.
10. Grave Hound - Ally. 2 HP. Exhaust: +1 damage if you attack the same target as another player.

Immediate scene cards
11. Quick Slash - Deal 1 damage.
12. Shield Breaker - Deal 2 damage.
13. Arc Bolt - Deal 2 damage to a magical or flying target, otherwise 1.
14. Battle Prayer - Another player gets +1 to a save or damaging action.
15. Dive for Cover - Prevent 2 damage from a hazard or scene backlash.
16. Field Bandage - Heal 2 party HP after the scene.

Greed, utility, and rescue cards
17. Haggle - In a merchant or social scene, gain 1 reward draw.
18. Smoke Route - Cancel one forced discard from a trap or collapse.
19. Mercy Prayer - Another player discards 1 fewer card; you draw 1.
20. Bold Claim - If you dealt the last damage to a monster, gain 1 extra Spotlight.

Opening Hands Used In This Simulation
- Kael: Sharpen Edge, Quick Slash, Bold Claim
- Brin: Treasure Nose, Haggle, Hidden Stash
- Mira: Tin Golem, Smoke Route, Map the Tunnels
- Tamsin: Cinder Familiar, Battle Prayer, Mercy Prayer
