Event Sequence For Quest 002

1. Camp at the Sealed Stair
- Prep scene only. No hostile threat.

2. Wandering Merchant
- Social scene. Players may trade, haggle, or scout for supplies.

3. Ash Imp Nest
- Danger scene. Spawn 2 Ash Imps.

4. Falling Causeway
- Hazard scene. Each player attempts a save or risks losing a card.

5. Shrine of Echoes
- Temptation scene. Players may invest in future power, but greed can create a persistent enchantment.

6. Bound Pilgrim
- Rescue scene. The party chooses whether to spend effort on someone who cannot pay them back.

7. Ashen Ambush
- Escalating danger scene.
- Low tension version: spawn 2 Goblin Skulkers.
- Mid tension version: spawn 2 Orc Raiders.
- High tension version: spawn 1 Ogre Brute.

8. Vault Gallery
- Trap and loot scene. Players can chase reward at the risk of losing cards.

9. Brood Wyrm Hunt
- Danger scene. Spawn 1 Brood Wyrm.

10. Dragon in the Deep
- Final scene. Resolve two boss stages in sequence if the party survives.
