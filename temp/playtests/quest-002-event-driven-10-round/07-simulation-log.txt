Dice results consumed in order: 4, 5, 3, 6, 1, 2, 6, 6, 3, 5, 2, 3, 1, 5

Starting state
- Party HP: 12
- Tension: 0

ROUND-01: Camp at the Sealed Stair
State at start: HP 12, tension 0
Scene:
- This is the prep round before the party enters the dungeon.
Play:
- Kael plays Sharpen Edge, announcing that the first thing he sees underground is already dead.
- Brin plays Treasure Nose and immediately asks whether treasure taken before a rescue still counts as "evil."
- Mira quietly assembles Tin Golem.
- Tamsin summons Cinder Familiar and says, "Good. Now at least one creature here listens."
Outcome:
- No Spotlight awarded. The prep beat does exactly what it should: players declare intention before danger starts.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Shield Breaker, Brin draws Pocket the Spoils, Mira draws Dive for Cover, Tamsin draws Field Bandage.
State at end: HP 12, tension 1

ROUND-02: Wandering Merchant
State at start: HP 12, tension 1
Scene:
- A smoke-stained merchant offers charms, rope, gossip, and prices that change depending on who looks desperate.
Play:
- Brin jumps in first with Haggle.
- d6 roll = 4, so the haggle succeeds.
- Tamsin uses Cinder Familiar to scout the next crate and quietly tips Kael toward a better blade oil, but Brin still takes center stage.
- Kael mutters that Brin managed to make the first real scene entirely about pockets.
Outcome:
- Brin claims 1 Spotlight.
- Treasure Nose triggers because Brin claimed the first reward of the run; he draws 2.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Grave Hound, Brin draws Bold Claim, Mira draws Hidden Stash, Tamsin draws Battle Prayer.
State at end: HP 12, tension 2

ROUND-03: Ash Imp Nest
State at start: HP 12, tension 2
Scene:
- The party opens a cracked furnace niche and two Ash Imps scramble out shrieking.
- Spawn: 2 Ash Imps, each HP 2, attack 1.
Play:
- Kael goes first with Shield Breaker. Sharpen Edge pushes it to 3 damage, deleting the first imp in one swing.
- Brin chooses Hidden Stash instead of helping, drawing deeper while everyone stares at him.
- Tamsin spends Battle Prayer on Mira, letting her guide the Tin Golem into the second imp.
- Mira exhausts Tin Golem to help pin the imp and Kael finishes it with Quick Slash.
Outcome:
- The party clears the nest without losing HP, but the emotional result matters more than the board state.
- Kael claims 1 Spotlight and openly tells Brin he built a bigger hand while the rest of the table fought a furnace full of knives.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Field Bandage, Brin draws Shield Breaker, Mira draws Study the Signs, Tamsin draws Mercy Prayer.
State at end: HP 12, tension 3

ROUND-04: Falling Causeway
State at start: HP 12, tension 3
Scene:
- The stone bridge flexes over lava vents. Each hero must make a save or lose a card to the collapse.
Play:
- Kael rolls 5 and keeps his footing.
- Brin rolls 3. Tamsin uses her passive to push it to 4, changing failure into success.
- Because her +1 made the difference, Tamsin rolls again and gets a 6. The card Brin would have lost slides into Tamsin's hand instead of the abyss.
- Brin says "Thank you" like it physically hurts him.
- Mira rolls 1 but uses Smoke Route to cancel the forced discard.
- Tamsin rolls 2 and loses Field Bandage to the lava.
Outcome:
- Tamsin claims 1 Spotlight. This is the run's first truly relationally charged beat.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Arc Bolt, Brin draws Arc Bolt, Mira draws Lantern Sprite, Tamsin draws Dive for Cover.
State at end: HP 12, tension 4

ROUND-05: Shrine of Echoes
State at start: HP 12, tension 4
Scene:
- A shrine offers power to anyone willing to leave something of themselves behind.
Play:
- Brin takes the greedy line and uses Pocket the Spoils, framing the shrine as free upside.
- Mira uses Study the Signs to read the room and avoids the obvious curse.
- Kael takes a minor boon for future damage because he wants the dragon, not the shrine, to remember him.
- Tamsin plays Mercy Prayer when Kael's boon would have forced a discard; he keeps the card, and she draws 1.
- Brin's line wakes Clinging Miasma, a persistent enchantment that will follow the party until dispelled.
Outcome:
- Brin claims 1 Spotlight because he manipulates the room into paying out more than it should.
- The scene pushes tension harder than a battle would have. Everyone learns that Brin will choose future advantage if the rules allow it.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Shield Breaker, Brin draws Hidden Stash, Mira draws Field Bandage, Tamsin draws Lantern Sprite.
- Tension reaches 5. The dragon stirs.
State at end: HP 12, tension 5

ROUND-06: Bound Pilgrim
State at start: HP 12, tension 5
Scene:
- A half-burned pilgrim begs to be cut loose from a ritual stake. Clinging Miasma is still in play, making the whole chamber feel poisoned.
Play:
- Brin asks whether the chains are made of anything saleable before he offers aid.
- The group realizes someone has to address the miasma first or it will keep stripping cards from the player with the biggest hand, which is currently Brin.
- Tamsin uses Cinder Familiar and her active to help Kael break the stake cleanly while Mira directs Tin Golem through the fumes to disperse the enchantment's last hold.
- Clinging Miasma is dispelled during the scene.
Outcome:
- The pilgrim is saved and the persistent enchantment is cleared.
- Tamsin claims 1 Spotlight.
- Because her active directly helped resolve the scene, Kael and Tamsin each draw 1.
- Kael does not thank her out loud, which somehow makes the moment feel more sincere.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Bold Claim, Brin draws Map the Tunnels, Mira draws Shield Breaker, Tamsin draws Quick Slash.
State at end: HP 12, tension 6

ROUND-07: Ashen Ambush
State at start: HP 12, tension 6
Scene:
- The corridor erupts into an ambush.
- Because tension is in the mid band, the event spawns 2 Orc Raiders instead of goblins.
Play:
- Kael opens on the first orc with Shield Breaker for 3 total damage after his passive, killing it cleanly.
- Mira backs him up with Tin Golem positioning while Brin waits for a better spotlight line.
- The second orc is nearly down when Brin fires Arc Bolt and then slaps down Bold Claim to take the last damage.
- Kael looks furious because Brin undeniably kill-stole the scene again.
Outcome:
- Brin claims 1 Spotlight.
- One orc swing still gets through in the scramble, forcing Brin to discard 1 card.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Battle Prayer, Brin draws Haggle, Mira draws Hidden Stash, Tamsin draws Arc Bolt.
State at end: HP 12, tension 7

ROUND-08: Vault Gallery
State at start: HP 12, tension 7
Scene:
- The gallery is all mirrors, locked chests, and a Heavy Door sealing the best reward behind it.
- Spawn: Heavy Door, durability 4. Until broken, the scene reward cannot be claimed.
Play:
- Brin immediately lunges for the reward line anyway and rolls 6 on the vault save.
- Kael and Mira are the ones who actually break the door: Kael hacks into the hinges while Mira directs Tin Golem through the frame.
- Kael then rolls 3 on the trap follow-up. Tamsin again uses her passive to turn it into a 4, and the follow-up salvage roll is 5, so the card he would have dropped gets pulled into Tamsin's hand instead.
Outcome:
- Brin still takes the visible loot once the door is open, which is technically fair and emotionally infuriating.
- Brin claims 1 Spotlight because he ends the scene with the relic, even though the others made it possible.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Quick Slash, Brin draws Pocket the Spoils, Mira draws Dive for Cover, Tamsin draws Field Bandage.
State at end: HP 12, tension 8

ROUND-09: Brood Wyrm Hunt
State at start: HP 12, tension 8
Scene:
- A Brood Wyrm drops from the ceiling in a rain of stone and molted scales.
- Spawn: 1 Brood Wyrm, HP 4, attack 2. On reveal, tension +1.
Play:
- The reveal pushes tension to 9.
- Kael commits first with Arc Bolt for 1 only, because the wyrm is armored rather than magical.
- Tamsin uses Battle Prayer on Kael to bring the hit up and keep him relevant.
- Mira exhausts Tin Golem to blunt the Brood Wyrm's counterattack, then finishes the setup with Shield Breaker.
- Brin hesitates, clearly hoping to line up another last-hit.
Outcome:
- Mira claims 1 Spotlight because the golem's body-block plus her follow-through are what actually stabilize the scene.
- Tin Golem is destroyed in the process, and that feels surprisingly sad at the table.
- The party still takes 1 damage from the tail sweep that gets past the golem.
- End-of-round draws: Kael draws Dive for Cover, Brin draws Quick Slash, Mira draws Lantern Sprite, Tamsin draws Mercy Prayer.
State at end: HP 11, tension 9

ROUND-10: Dragon in the Deep
State at start: HP 11, tension 9
Scene:
- The chains snap. The dragon arrives as a two-step fight: first the Chainbound Head, then the Heartfire body behind it.
- Stage 1: Chainbound Head, HP 5, attack 2.
- Stage 2: Heartfire Dragon, HP 6, attack 3. On reveal, each player either discards 1 or the party takes 1.
Stage 1 play:
- Kael commits first with Shield Breaker for 3 after his passive.
- Brin swings in with Quick Slash and tries to set up Bold Claim.
- Tamsin uses her active on Kael for +1 damage.
- Mira spends her last Ward to prevent the head's lash damage from softening the party before stage 2.
- Kael lands the finishing hit on stage 1 and gains the extra Spotlight from his aggressive active.
Stage 2 play:
- Heartfire Dragon appears.
- Kael chooses to discard 1 to keep party HP intact.
- Brin refuses to discard and makes the table eat 1 party damage instead.
- Mira uses Dive for Cover to prevent 2 of the incoming 3 attack when the dragon breathes.
- Tamsin plays Mercy Prayer to save Kael from losing another card and draws 1.
- Brin tries to set up a final Bold Claim line, but Tamsin chooses Kael for Battle Prayer instead.
- Kael lands the last damage on the dragon while Brin actually makes the math possible with a late Arc Bolt.
Outcome:
- Kael claims 2 Spotlight for the final scene and gets the heroic story beat he wanted.
- Brin immediately points out that his damage was still essential, which is true and makes the victory feel properly messy.
- The party survives on 10 HP.

Immediate feel summary
- Prep made the opening breathe.
- Non-combat scenes produced more interesting feelings than pure repeated fighting.
- Tamsin's save-flip ability created the most memorable moments in the whole run.
- Brin using build cards during danger scenes produced exactly the kind of resentment the design seems to want.
- The dragon scene felt more dramatic with two stages than the previous one-roll boss.
- The ambush scaling cleanly from the same blurb to different enemies felt right.
- Persistent cards like miasma and the heavy door made the dungeon feel more like a place and less like a sequence of isolated tests.
