Quest: Ashen Depths, Event-Driven Pass
Theme: Classic fantasy dungeon descent

Premise:
The party camps outside the sealed stair at dusk, choosing what kind of heroes they intend to be before they descend. Inside the ruin, each chamber is a story beat first and a mechanical problem second.

Quest Track:
- Tension 0: prep scene outside the dungeon
- Tension 5: "The dragon stirs"
- Tension 10: Dragon in the Deep

Quest Trait:
- The table begins with one prep round before entering the dungeon.
- During the prep round, each player may play one setup card or summon one allied entity.

Midpoint Effect:
- Once tension reaches 5, danger scenes are more likely to spawn persistent enemies, harsher discard effects, or stronger versions of the same encounter.

Lose Condition:
- If shared party HP reaches 0, everyone loses.

Win Condition For This Simulation:
- Survive all 10 rounds and resolve the dragon's two-step final scene.
