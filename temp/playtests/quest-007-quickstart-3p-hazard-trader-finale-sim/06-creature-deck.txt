Instruction:
- Spawn 1 Threat 2 humanoid and 2 Threat 1 beasts. Also spawn Iron Gate.

Creature-deck order segment used for criteria:
1. Orc Raider: Threat 2 humanoid
2. Ember Bat: Threat 1 beast
3. Gray Wolf: Threat 1 beast

Friendly Creature Targeting Notes
- Cinder Familiar absorbed the first Goblin Chieftain attack and was defeated.
- Tin Golem absorbed several later enemy attacks because enemy monsters must target a legal friendly creature before attacking the party.
- Tin Golem's `Exhaust: Prevent 1 damage to the party` mattered only when damage would hit the party; it did not prevent damage to itself.
