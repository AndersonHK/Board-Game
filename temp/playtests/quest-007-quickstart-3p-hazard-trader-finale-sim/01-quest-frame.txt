Quest: Ashen Depths, Quickstart-First Three-Player Hazard/Trader/Finale Run
Theme: Classic fantasy dungeon descent through ash-choked ruins

Purpose:
Run a fresh three-player simulated game from the current quickstart and live card files, with special attention on what real players can execute from the quickstart, cards, and widgets when the session includes hazards, a trader scene, carried Ash Miasma, reward-draw taxes, criteria spawning, and the final ordeal.

Premise:
Three adventurers enter the Ashen Depths with the player-facing quickstart sheet, printed cards, and table widgets. They do not have design docs, prior playtest notes, or hidden designer intent at the table.

Quest Card Used:
- Ashen Depths
- Player count: 3
- Starting party HP: 18
- Starting hand size: 4
- Escalation track: 0 to 10

Quest Thresholds Used:
- Escalation 0: prep scene instead of a hostile battle
- Threshold 5: after the current scene ends, resolve Furnace Warden Rises
- Threshold 5 ongoing rider: starting with the next scene, the first player each scene to gain an extra draw from a reward must discard 1 card after that reward resolves
- Threshold 8: in hostile scenes, the first hostile creature attack each scene gets +1 Attack
- Threshold 10: after the current scene ends, reveal Dragon in the Deep; the normal two-turn round cap is removed

Widgets Used:
- one red d20 tracking party HP from 18
- one escalation tracker from 0 to 10
- red damage tokens for damageable creatures and artifacts
- blue shield tokens for shield effects
- one d6 for saves, tests, Haggle, Warrior passive rolls, and Dragon's Breath

Lose Condition:
- If party HP reaches 0, everyone loses immediately.

Win Condition For This Simulation:
- Reach escalation 10, survive Dragon in the Deep, and defeat Heartfire Dragon.

Session Intent:
- use one Warrior, one Wizard, and one Cleric
- use the live random encounter library as the shuffled pool
- obey the current "finish the round structure even after the scene clears" rule
- note every missing table instruction or undefined phrase that a real group would have to resolve
