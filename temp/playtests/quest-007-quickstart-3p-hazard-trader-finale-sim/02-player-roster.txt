Simulation Roster: Three Players, Three Unique Classes

1. Merek Ashford
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants to be the visible finisher and collect the most dangerous trophies
- Quick-start passive: when Merek deals the final point of damage to a monster with his own action card, creature, or Warrior trait damage, roll 1d6 and draw 1 if the result is less than or equal to `monster threat x rarity multiplier`
- Quick-start active: once per turn, Merek may deal `2` damage
- Hidden priority: claim hostile cards, especially elites and bosses

2. Selene Voss
- Class: Wizard
- Persona: wants to outlast the ruin and turn patience into the largest personal pile
- Quick-start passive: hand limit `12`
- Quick-start active: once per scene, place `2` shield tokens on the party or any creature
- Hidden priority: keep high Treasure Value cards and own durable permanents on the field

3. Brother Oren
- Class: Cleric
- Persona: wants to look indispensable while scoring support moments others cannot deny
- Quick-start passive: once per scene, when another player makes a save, give that roll `+1`; if that flips failure into success, that player chooses `1` card they would have lost and puts it into Oren's hand instead
- Quick-start active: once per scene, heal `1` party HP or `2` HP to any creature
- Hidden priority: create support-enabled successes and keep the party alive just enough to win

Starting Hand Notes:
- All players use the quest card's three-player starting hand size of `4`.
- Merek and Oren use the baseline hand limit of `6`.
- Selene uses the Wizard hand limit of `12` from the start of the game.
- The roster intentionally stresses three prestige routes: hostile Glory, retained Treasure, and support-agenda value.
