Agenda Draws

Merek Ashford
- Agenda: Ashes to Ashes
- Goal Text: Succeed if the total Treasure Value of hostile cards you claimed is 10 or higher.
- Reward Text: Gain 20 prestige points.
- Why it fits this run: Merek is incentivized to chase elite hostile claims and final hits, even when the party might prefer cleaner shared problem solving.

Selene Voss
- Agenda: Prepared Beyond Reason
- Goal Text: Succeed if you end the game with at least 12 Treasure Value in hand and on-field owned cards.
- Reward Text: Gain 20 prestige points.
- Why it fits this run: Selene tests whether the Wizard's `12` hand limit and permanent ownership route can score without needing the richest-at-table agenda.

Brother Oren
- Agenda: By My Grace
- Goal Text: Succeed if you cause 3 shared-success moments or 2 failed saves to become successes.
- Reward Text: Gain 20 prestige points.
- Why it fits this run: Oren tests whether the phrase `shared-success moments` is concrete enough for real players to adjudicate from card text.
