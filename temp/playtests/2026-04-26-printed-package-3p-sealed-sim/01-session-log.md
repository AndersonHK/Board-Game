# Session Log

## Round 1: Prep Scene, Escalation 0

The table began with the quest's prep scene. No hostile or blocking hurdle appeared.

Actions:

- Mira paid 1 party HP to play Summon Grave Hound. Party HP fell to 17.
- Sol played Warding Circle.
- Brann passed because no printed prep action was obviously useful.
- Each player took the standard draw.
- Sol played Prepared Ground.

End of round:

- Warding Circle placed 2 shield tokens on the party.
- The prep scene cleared and escalation advanced to 1.

Temporary ruling:

- Passing in a non-hostile prep scene is allowed when a player has no meaningful action. The quickstart says players take actions "if able" but does not explicitly define pass.

## Round 2: Soot-Stall Trader, Escalation 1

Reveal: the top 5 resource cards became trader stock.

Trader interactions:

- Mira direct-traded Chain Cutter for Blood Price Burst.
- Sol direct-traded Dispel Draft for Summon Cinder Familiar.
- Brann haggled, rolled 6, and traded Mercy Prayer for Field Bandage and Chain Cutter.

The scene cleared once all three players traded.

Later actions:

- Each player took the standard draw.
- Sol summoned Cinder Familiar.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 more party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 2.
- Unused trader stock was discarded.

Temporary ruling:

- Each trader interaction used the player's action. The trader rules say each player gets exactly one interaction, but the printed text does not say whether trading consumes an action or happens outside the round.

## Round 3: Cracked Causeway, Escalation 2

Reveal: each player saved.

- Mira rolled 3 and would fail.
- Sol rolled 4 and succeeded.
- Brann rolled 2 and failed.
- Brann used Cleric Rescue on Mira. The +1 changed Mira's roll to 4, so Mira chose the card she would have lost and Brann put that card into hand.
- Brann lost 1 card for his own failed save.

Actions:

- Mira spent 1 action to secure the crossing.
- The crossing cleared.
- Mira scouted the next encounter card: Kennel Vault.
- Each player took the standard draw.
- Brann played Field Bandage after the scene cleared, healing party HP from 17 to 18.
- Sol played Hidden Stash and drew 3 cards.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 3.

Temporary rulings:

- The crossing was secured automatically by spending the action because the encounter does not say to roll.
- The failed player chose which card was lost. The printed card says "lose 1 card" but not who chooses or whether it is random.
- Party HP was capped at starting HP.

## Round 4: Kennel Vault, Escalation 3

Reveal: the table spawned Orc Raider, Gray Wolf, Ember Bat, and Iron Gate.

Actions:

- Sol used Wizard active to add 2 party shields.
- Mira paid 1 party HP and played Blood Price Burst to deal 5 damage to Orc Raider. Orc Raider was defeated and claimed by Mira. Warrior Kill Reward succeeded on a roll of 2, so Mira drew 1 card.
- Brann used Lockpick Kit against Iron Gate, rolled 2 + 3, succeeded, cleared Iron Gate, and claimed it.

Enemy Turn 1:

- Gray Wolf attacked Grave Hound for 2 damage. The wolf's on-hit discard did not apply because Sol's shield active counted as a support effect this turn.
- Ember Bat attacked Cinder Familiar for 2 damage, defeating it.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

Actions:

- Mira played Quick Slash to defeat Ember Bat and claimed it. Warrior Kill Reward failed.
- Brann played Arc Bolt on Gray Wolf for 2 damage.
- Sol played Arc Bolt on Gray Wolf for 2 more damage, defeated it, and claimed it.

End of round:

- The scene cleared. Sol cleared the last blocker and gained 1 extra resource draw.
- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 4.

Temporary rulings:

- Lockpick Kit could clear Iron Gate with a successful door/vault test even though Iron Gate only prints HP and combat targetability.
- Enemy targeting among multiple legal friendly creatures was chosen by the table.
- Wizard active counted as a support effect for Gray Wolf's on-hit exception.

## Round 5: Reliquary of Cinders, Escalation 4

Reveal: Blessing Brazier entered under party control and Ash Miasma entered hostile.

Actions:

- Sol used Dispel Draft to remove Ash Miasma and claimed it as the player who removed the hostile card.
- Brann spent an action to claim the encounter reward, gaining 1 extra draw.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 5.
- Threshold 5 was reached, so Furnace Warden Rises would resolve after this scene.

Temporary rulings:

- Removing Ash Miasma with Dispel Draft counts as claiming the hostile enchantment.
- The reward required a separate action because the clear condition says "reward claimed" and the quickstart says rewards with no timing can be claimed by spending an action.
- The threshold-5 reward tax did not apply yet because the quest says "starting with the next scene."

## Round 6: Furnace Warden Rises, Escalation 5

Reveal: Ashen Warden spawned.

Actions:

- Brann played Battle Prayer on Mira for +2 damage.
- Mira discarded 1 card for Shield Breaker and dealt 7 damage to Ashen Warden.
- Sol played Arc Bolt for 2 damage.

Enemy Turn 1:

- Ashen Warden used Furnace Sweep, removing all party shields, then attacked as normal.
- Prepared Ground reduced the first hostile creature attack by 2.
- Ashen Warden attacked Grave Hound, dealing the final 1 damage needed to defeat it.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

Actions:

- Mira used Warrior active to deal 2 damage, defeating Ashen Warden and claiming it.
- Warrior Kill Reward succeeded automatically on the roll window for a Threat 3 Elite, so Mira drew 1 card.
- Furnace Warden Rises reward made each player draw 1 card. The threshold-5 reward tax hit Mira as the first player in fixed order to gain the extra encounter reward draw; Mira discarded 1 card after drawing.
- Brann played Field Bandage after the scene cleared, healing party HP to 18.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 6.

Temporary rulings:

- Battle Prayer modified Shield Breaker's card damage.
- Ashen Warden's attack targeted a friendly creature before the party.
- Simultaneous encounter reward draws were resolved in fixed player order for the threshold-5 tax.
- A scripted ordeal counted as a new scene for escalation advancement.

## Round 7: Ember-Bell Nursery, Escalation 6

Reveal: 2 Ember Bats and 1 Ash Cultist spawned. Ash Cultist's on-reveal discard hit Sol, who had the largest hand.

Actions:

- Sol played Arc Bolt to defeat one Ember Bat and claimed it.
- Mira played Quick Slash to defeat the second Ember Bat and claimed it. Warrior Kill Reward succeeded on a roll of 1, so Mira drew 1 card.
- Brann played Arc Bolt on Ash Cultist for 2 damage.

Enemy Turn 1:

- Ash Cultist attacked the party.
- Prepared Ground reduced the first hostile creature attack by 2, so the attack dealt 0.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

Actions:

- Mira used Warrior active to defeat Ash Cultist and claimed it. Warrior Kill Reward failed.

End of round:

- The scene cleared in 1 round, so each player could keep 1 extra card at cleanup.
- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 7.

Temporary ruling:

- Ash Cultist's "one player discards" target was chosen by table consensus, using largest hand as the fairest visible selector.

## Round 8: Smoke-Flood Gallery, Escalation 7

Reveal: each player chose discard 1 card or make the party take 1 damage.

- Mira and Sol chose discard.
- Brann played Smoke Route to cancel both chosen discards.
- Brann chose party damage for his own choice. Party HP fell from 18 to 17.

Actions:

- Sol played Study the Signs for +3 to his next non-combat test, rolled 2 + 3, and vented the gallery.
- Sol returned 1 card from the discard pile to hand.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 8.
- Threshold 8 became active for future hostile scenes.

Temporary rulings:

- Smoke Route could cancel chosen discards from Smoke-Flood Gallery even though the effect offers a damage alternative.
- Venting used the default 4+ non-combat test because no number is printed.
- Returning a card from discard to hand is not an "extra draw," so threshold 5 did not tax it.

## Round 9: Kennel Break, Escalation 8

Reveal: Goblin Chieftain, Goblin Bruiser, and Goblin Cutthroat spawned.

Actions:

- Brann played Battle Prayer on Mira for +2 damage.
- Mira paid 1 party HP and played Blood Price Burst to deal 7 damage to Goblin Chieftain, defeating and claiming it. Warrior Kill Reward succeeded.
- Sol played Arc Bolt on Goblin Bruiser. Goblin Bruiser survived damage and gained +2 Attack until end of round.

Enemy Turn 1:

- Goblin Bruiser made the first hostile creature attack of the scene, so threshold 8 added +1 Attack.
- Prepared Ground reduced that same attack by 2.
- Party shields absorbed the final 3 damage.
- Goblin Cutthroat attacked next, removing the last shield and dealing 1 party HP damage.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

Actions:

- Mira played Quick Slash to defeat Goblin Bruiser and claimed it. Warrior Kill Reward succeeded.
- Sol played Arc Bolt on Goblin Cutthroat.
- Brann made a basic attack for 1 damage, defeated Goblin Cutthroat, and claimed it.
- Kennel Break rewarded the player who defeated the last hostile with 1 extra resource draw. Brann drew 1 card, then discarded 1 card for the threshold-5 tax.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 9.

Temporary rulings:

- Threshold 8 and Prepared Ground both modified the first hostile attack; the table applied both.
- Threshold 5 taxed only the first player to gain an extra draw from encounter Reward Text, not Warrior Kill Reward.

## Round 10: Bound Pilgrim, Escalation 9

Reveal: Rusted Chains spawned.

Actions:

- Brann used Chain Cutter on Rusted Chains, rolled 1 + 3, succeeded, cleared the restraint, and claimed it.
- Bound Pilgrim healed the party 1 HP, from 15 to 16.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

End of round:

- Warding Circle added 2 party shields.
- Escalation advanced to 10.
- Threshold 10 triggered Dragon in the Deep after the current scene ended.

Temporary rulings:

- Chain Cutter could clear Rusted Chains with a restraint test even though the artifact also has HP and combat targetability.
- The Bound Pilgrim reward healing was capped by the party HP maximum ruling.

## Final Ordeal: Dragon in the Deep, Escalation 10

Reveal: Chainbound Head spawned. Heartfire Dragon could not be targeted until Chainbound Head was defeated.

Actions:

- Brann played Battle Prayer on Mira for +2 damage.
- Mira discarded 1 card for Shield Breaker and dealt 7 damage to Chainbound Head, defeating and claiming it.
- Heartfire Dragon spawned immediately.

Enemy Turn 1:

- Heartfire Dragon used Dragon's Breath and still attacked.
- Mira chose the discard roll and rolled 2, discarding no cards.
- Sol chose party damage. The table rolled d6 as d3 and got 5, treated as 3 damage.
- Brann chose the discard roll and rolled 4, discarding 1 card.
- The dragon attacked for 4, plus 1 from threshold 8, minus 2 from Prepared Ground. Party shields absorbed the final 3 damage.

Standard draw: each player drew 1 card.

Actions:

- Sol played Arc Bolt for 2 damage to Heartfire Dragon.
- Mira played Last Step for 2 damage to Heartfire Dragon.
- Brann played Quick Slash for 2 damage to Heartfire Dragon.

Enemy Turn 2:

- Heartfire Dragon used Dragon's Breath again.
- Mira chose party damage and rolled d3 for 1 damage.
- Sol chose the discard roll and discarded no cards.
- Brann chose party damage and rolled d3 for 2 damage.
- Heartfire Dragon attacked for 4 damage.
- Party HP fell from 16 to 9 after breath and attack damage.

Extra actions:

- Mira used Warrior active for 2 damage, bringing Heartfire Dragon to 8 damage.
- Sol paid 1 party HP for Blood Price Burst, dealt 5 damage, defeated Heartfire Dragon, and claimed it.

Result:

- Party survived at 8 HP.
- The players won the quest.

Temporary rulings:

- Heartfire Dragon could use Dragon's Breath on every enemy turn because no cooldown is printed.
- d3 meant d6 divided by two, rounded up.
- Final-ordeal extra turns were played as one action per player followed by one enemy turn.
- No extra round-end cleanup or Warding Circle trigger happened during final extra turns because the printed final-ordeal text only says to alternate extra player turns and enemy turns.
