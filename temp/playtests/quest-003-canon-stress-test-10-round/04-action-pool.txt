Action / Resource Pool: Canon Stress Prototype Catalog

Important note:
This remains a prototype catalog, not a one-copy finalized deck list.

Setup and engine cards
1. Sharpen Edge - Ongoing. Your next damaging action gets +1 damage.
2. Treasure Nose - Ongoing. The first time you claim a reward, draw 2.
3. Map the Tunnels - Ongoing. Later, reroll one save.
4. Pocket the Spoils - If a reward is claimed this scene, you gain 1 extra draw.
5. Hidden Stash - Draw 2.
6. Study the Signs - Peek ahead or gain +1 to your next non-combat test.
7. Ward Sigil - Ongoing. Gain 1 Ward.
8. Chain Cutter - Gain +2 to break an obstacle or free a captive.

Summons and allies
9. Cinder Familiar - Ally. 1 HP. Exhaust: +1 to a magical or support action.
10. Tin Golem - Ally. 3 HP. Exhaust: prevent 1 damage to the party.
11. Lantern Sprite - Ally. 1 HP. Exhaust: another player draws 1, then discards 1.
12. Grave Hound - Ally. 2 HP. Exhaust: +1 damage if you attack the same target as another player.

Immediate scene cards
13. Quick Slash - Deal 1 damage.
14. Shield Breaker - Deal 2 damage to a creature or 2 durability to an obstacle.
15. Arc Bolt - Deal 2 damage to a magical or flying target, otherwise 1.
16. Battle Prayer - Another player gets +1 to a save or damaging action.
17. Dive for Cover - Prevent 2 damage from a hazard or creature attack.
18. Field Bandage - Heal 2 party HP after the scene.

Greed, utility, and rescue cards
19. Smoke Route - Cancel one forced discard or trap loss.
20. Mercy Prayer - Another player discards 1 fewer card; you draw 1.
21. Lockpick Kit - Gain +2 to a vault, door, or restraint test.
22. Dispel Draft - Deal 2 durability to an enchantment or ongoing curse.
23. Bold Claim - If you removed the last hostile creature this scene, gain 1 extra Spotlight.
24. Rally Cry - All allies may exhaust immediately this turn.
25. Haggle - In a merchant or social scene, gain 1 reward draw or +2 on the bargain test.

Opening Hands Used In This Simulation
- Kael: Sharpen Edge, Shield Breaker, Bold Claim
- Brin: Treasure Nose, Hidden Stash, Pocket the Spoils
- Mira: Tin Golem, Smoke Route, Map the Tunnels
- Tamsin: Cinder Familiar, Battle Prayer, Mercy Prayer
