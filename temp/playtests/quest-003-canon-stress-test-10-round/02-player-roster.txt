Simulation Roster: Same Four Friends, Sharper Canon Stress

1. Kael Stonehand
- Archetype: Aggressive
- Persona: wants the dungeon to remember that he went first
- Passive: the first damaging action you take each scene gets +1 damage if you committed before anyone else
- Active: once per scene, Press the Advantage for +1 damage; if that action resolves the last hostile creature or blocking hurdle, gain 1 extra Spotlight

2. Brin Ashvale
- Archetype: Aggressive
- Persona: wants the loot story even more than the hero story
- Passive: the first damaging action you take each scene gets +1 damage if you committed before anyone else
- Active: once per scene, Press the Advantage for +1 damage; if that action resolves the last hostile creature or blocking hurdle, gain 1 extra Spotlight

3. Mira Fen
- Archetype: Defensive
- Persona: refuses to be caught empty-handed or surprised
- Starts with: 1 Ward
- Passive: once per scene, when an event would make you discard or take damage, prevent 1 of that loss
- Active: spend 1 Ward or discard a setup card to prevent 2 party damage or ignore 1 forced discard

4. Tamsin Vale
- Archetype: Support
- Persona: wants everyone to need her help, even when that help comes with a memory attached
- Passive: once per scene, give +1 to another player's save roll; if that flips a failure into a success, roll a d6 and on 4-6 move one card that would have been lost into your own hand instead
- Active: once per scene, give another player +1 damage or +1 to a non-combat test; if that help resolves the last blocking hurdle, both players draw 1
