Quest: Ashen Depths, Canon Stress Test Pass
Theme: Classic fantasy dungeon descent

Purpose:
Stress recently canonized rules from `defines/` while staying inside the same fantasy vertical-slice frame used in Quest 002.

Premise:
The party returns to the Ashen Depths with clearer rules but harsher pressure. This pass assumes the dungeon is not only dangerous but also paced: clearing chambers advances the descent, while unfinished trouble makes the whole ruin destabilize faster.

Quest Track:
- Tension 0: prep scene at the sealed stair
- Tension 5: the furnaces wake and greed starts costing more
- Tension 10: Dragon in the Deep unlocks as the final ordeal

Quest Traits Used In This Run:
- The first round is a prep scene, but it still uses the standard two-turn round structure.
- Fully clearing a new scene advances baseline tension by 1.
- Unresolved hostile or blocking hurdles add the canonical +1 escalation at end of round.
- The final ordeal begins only after tension 10 has been reached.

Lose Condition:
- If shared party HP reaches 0, everyone loses.

Win Condition For This Simulation:
- Survive the descent and clear both boss stages after escalation 10 is reached.

Stress Points Built Into This Package:
- one exact-name spawn scene
- one criteria-based spawn scene that depends on creature-deck order
- one persistent enchantment
- one multi-round encounter with no repeated default mid-encounter draw
- one escalation-10 ordeal that continues beyond the normal two-turn cap
