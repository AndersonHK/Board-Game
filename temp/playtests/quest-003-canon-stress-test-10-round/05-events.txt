Event Sequence For Quest 003

1. Camp at the Sealed Stair
- Prep scene only.
- No hostile spawns, but the standard round structure still applies.

2. Wandering Merchant
- Social scene.
- Players may haggle, scout, or take selfish setup lines.

3. Kennel Break
- Exact-name danger scene.
- Spawn 1 Goblin Chieftain and 2 Goblin Warriors.

4. Falling Causeway
- Hazard scene.
- Each player attempts a save or loses a card to the collapse.

5. Shrine of Echoes
- Temptation scene.
- Greedy play can awaken Clinging Miasma as a persistent enchantment.

6. Bound Pilgrim
- Rescue scene.
- Chains count as a blocking hurdle.

7. Caged Hunt
- Criteria-based danger scene.
- Spawn 1 Strength 2 humanoid and 2 Strength 1 beasts by creature-deck order.
- Heavy Door also seals the exit and reward.

8. Continue Caged Hunt If Unresolved
- Do not reveal a new encounter.
- Do not grant another default mid-encounter draw.
- Clear remaining creatures and blocking cards before the descent can continue.

9. Brood Wyrm Hunt
- Danger scene.
- Spawn 1 Brood Wyrm.
- On reveal, tension +1.

10. Dragon in the Deep
- Final ordeal.
- Resolve Chainbound Head first, then Heartfire Dragon.
- At escalation 10, the normal two-turn cap no longer applies.
