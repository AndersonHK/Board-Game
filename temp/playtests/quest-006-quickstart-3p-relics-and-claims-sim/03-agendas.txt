Secret Agendas Used For This Run

Assignments

1. Garrick Stonehand -> Ashes to Ashes
- Goal Text: succeed if the total Treasure Value of hostile cards you claimed is 10 or higher
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

2. Lyra Vale -> Richer Than the Ruin
- Goal Text: succeed if you finish with the highest total Treasure Value in hand plus on-field owned cards
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

3. Sister Anwen -> Keeper of Relics
- Goal Text: succeed if you end the game owning at least 2 artifacts, enchantments, or summons on the field
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

Why This Mix Was Chosen
- Garrick's agenda forces the run to care about a real hostile-claim procedure.
- Lyra's agenda stresses the difference between claimed hostile cards and hand-plus-field Treasure.
- Anwen's agenda pressures ownership rules for field permanents, especially because this session also uses Blessing Brazier and multiple summons or enchantments.
