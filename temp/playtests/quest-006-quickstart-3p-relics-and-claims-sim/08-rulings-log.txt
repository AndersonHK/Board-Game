Rulings Log For Quest 006

RUL-01 - Claiming printed scene rewards
Issue:
- Reliquary of Cinders requires the chamber reward to be claimed, but the printed materials do not formalize a default "claim reward" action.
Smallest ruling used:
- If a scene clearly has a claimable reward and no other procedure is printed, a player may spend an action to claim that reward once the blocking conditions are gone.
Why this was minimal:
- It adds no new widget and uses the same action economy players already understand.
Impact on the run:
- Reliquary of Cinders became fully playable without inventing a new subsystem.
Promotion target:
- quick start encounter-actions wording or encounter glossary

RUL-02 - Hostile and blocking claim procedure
Issue:
- Prestige scoring names hostile and blocking cards you claimed, but the player-facing materials still do not explain who claims those cards.
Smallest ruling used:
- The player whose damage or clearing action removes the final point of resistance from a hostile or blocking card claims it.
Why this was minimal:
- It matches the most intuitive "who finished it" reading while covering both combat kills and cleared blockers.
Impact on the run:
- Garrick's Ashes to Ashes agenda, Lyra's Iron Gate claim, and final prestige scoring all became executable.
Promotion target:
- quick start prestige section

RUL-03 - Party-controlled versus player-owned permanents
Issue:
- Blessing Brazier enters under party control, but scoring and agendas care about player-owned cards on the field.
Smallest ruling used:
- Party-controlled permanents stay on the shared field but are not counted as player-owned unless a card explicitly gives one player control.
Why this was minimal:
- It avoids silently gifting shared cards to one player for prestige.
Impact on the run:
- Keeper of Relics stayed readable, and Blessing Brazier did not accidentally swing endgame scoring.
Promotion target:
- quick start prestige example
