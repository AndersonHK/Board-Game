Rulings Log For Quest 004

RUL-01 - Duplicate class selection in a four-player game
Issue:
- The quick start currently lists `Warrior`, `Wizard`, and `Cleric`, but the immediate real table size is four players.
Smallest ruling used:
- Duplicate classes are allowed, so this run used two Warriors, one Wizard, and one Cleric.
Why this was minimal:
- It made the existing quick-start package playable for four humans without inventing a fourth class.
Impact on the run:
- The duplicate-Warrior rivalry again created strong selfish-pressure evidence.
Promotion target:
- quick start

RUL-02 - Action order inside each action turn
Issue:
- The quick start says each player takes 1 action per turn, but it does not say whether order is fixed, rotating, or freely chosen.
Smallest ruling used:
- The table chooses the order of actions inside each action turn.
Why this was minimal:
- It required no new widget and let support timing matter without inventing initiative stats.
Impact on the run:
- Tamsin could boost the right player at the right moment, and selfish delays became visible behavior instead of just scripted narration.
Promotion target:
- design doc or quick start

RUL-03 - Temporary scene-credit tracker
Issue:
- The quick start confirms hidden agendas and prestige pressure, but it does not yet define a playable endgame credit or tie-break procedure.
Smallest ruling used:
- Keep using temporary Spotlight as a simulation-only prestige proxy: the player who removes the last hostile creature or blocking hurdle gets the scene credit unless the solve is genuinely inseparable.
Why this was minimal:
- It reuses prior precedent without pretending the scoring system is finalized.
Impact on the run:
- Agenda pressure stayed legible, but this is still not ready as player-facing canon.
Promotion target:
- keep temporary

RUL-05 - Creature reserve refresh between scenes

Why this was minimal:

Impact on the run:

RUL-06 - Escalation 10 reached during a non-final scene

Smallest ruling used:
- Finish the current scene first, then reveal the final ordeal on the next encounter.
Why this was minimal:
- It prevents a mid-scene interruption while still respecting the quest's escalation-10 endpoint.
Impact on the run:
- The transition into the dragon fight felt cleaner and easier for a real table to execute.
Promotion target:
- design doc or quick start

RUL-07 - Wizard forced-discard protection target
Issue:

Smallest ruling used:

Why this was minimal:
- It follows the plain-language reading without adding a new target restriction.
Impact on the run:

Promotion target:

RUL-08 - Furnace-smoke threshold timing
Issue:

Why this was minimal:
