Secret Agenda Deck: Tightened Prestige Prototype Set

Important note:
- The quick start confirms that each player takes a hidden agenda, but it does not yet define a finished prestige system.
- This package therefore keeps using temporary hidden objectives plus scene-credit tracking as a simulation stand-in.

Agenda Templates
- First Name in the Ballad: gain 4 prestige if you land the final blow on the final ordeal or finish with the most danger-scene Spotlight
- Richer Than the Ruin: gain 4 prestige if you generated at least 4 extra draws or reward claims and no other player generated more
- Prepared Beyond Reason: gain 4 prestige if you finish with at least 6 cards in hand or 2 persistent assets, and no other player has more stored value
- By My Grace: gain 4 prestige if your help flips 2 allied failures into successes or directly creates 3 shared-success draw moments

Assignments Used In This Run
- Kael -> First Name in the Ballad
- Brin -> Richer Than the Ruin
- Mira -> Prepared Beyond Reason
- Tamsin -> By My Grace
