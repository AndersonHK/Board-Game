Simulation Roster: Four Players, Three Classes, One Quick Start Sheet

1. Kael Stonehand
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants the room to remember that he struck first
- Quick-start passive: when he deals the final point of damage to a monster with his own card, creature, or Warrior trait damage, roll 1d6 and draw 1 if the result is less than or equal to `monster threat x rarity multiplier`
- Quick-start active: once per turn, deal 1 damage
- Hidden priority: wants danger-scene credit and the boss finish

2. Brin Ashvale
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants the loot story even more than the hero story
- Quick-start passive: same Warrior kill-reward rule as Kael
- Quick-start active: once per turn, deal 1 damage
- Hidden priority: wants extra draws, reward cards, and profitable last hits

3. Mira Fen
- Class: Wizard
- Persona: wants to survive with more options than anyone else
- Quick-start passive: hand limit `12`
- Quick-start active: once per scene, discard 1 card to either prevent 2 party damage or ignore 1 forced discard
- Hidden priority: wants to finish stocked, calm, and difficult to corner

4. Tamsin Vale
- Class: Cleric
- Persona: wants to be the reason everyone else is still standing
- Quick-start passive: once per scene, when another player makes a save, give that roll `+1`; if that flips failure into success, that player chooses 1 card they would have lost and puts it into Tamsin's hand instead
- Quick-start active: once per scene, give another player `+1` to card/trait damage or a non-combat test; if that bonus helps resolve the last blocking hurdle in the scene, both players draw 1
- Hidden priority: wants public gratitude and private card flow

Starting Hand Notes:
- All players start with 3 cards as required by canon and the quick start.
- Kael and Brin each use the normal hand limit of 6.
- Mira uses the quick-start Wizard hand limit of 12 from the start of the game.
- Tamsin uses the normal hand limit of 6.

Simulation-Only Note:
- This run treats duplicate class selection as legal because the immediate classroom table size is four but the quick start currently names only three classes.
