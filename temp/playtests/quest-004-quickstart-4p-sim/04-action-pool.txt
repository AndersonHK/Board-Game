Action / Resource Pool: Quickstart-Facing Prototype Catalog

Important note:
- This remains a prototype catalog, not a one-copy finalized deck list.
- Rarity is shown on player cards here because the quick start now teaches `Common` and `Elite` as shared card language.

Setup and utility cards
1. Hidden Stash - Common
- Draw 2.

2. Treasure Nose - Elite
- Ongoing.
- The first time you claim a reward each scene, draw 1.

3. Study the Signs - Common
- Peek at the next encounter or gain +1 to your next non-combat test.

4. Chain Cutter - Common
- Gain +2 on a chain, gate, or rubble test.

5. Smoke Route - Common
- Cancel 1 forced discard.

6. Warded Satchel - Elite
- Ignore 1 forced discard this scene.
- If no forced discard happens, draw 1 at cleanup.

7. Field Bandage - Common
- Heal 2 party HP after the scene.

8. Lockpick Kit - Common
- Gain +2 on a door, vault, or restraint test.

Combat and defense cards
9. Quick Slash - Common
- Deal 1 damage.

10. Shield Breaker - Elite
- Discard 1 card.
- Deal 3 damage to a creature or blocking obstacle.

11. Arc Bolt - Common
- Deal 2 damage to a flying or magical target, otherwise 1.

12. Battle Prayer - Common
- Another player gets +1 to damage or a non-combat test this turn.

13. Dive for Cover - Common
- Prevent 2 damage from a hazard or creature attack.

14. Rally Push - Elite
- Two players each get +1 damage this turn.

15. Last Step - Elite
- Deal 1 damage.
- If this defeats a monster or clears the last blocking hurdle, draw 1.

Support, greed, and ally cards
16. Mercy Prayer - Common
- Another player discards 1 fewer card.
- You draw 1.

17. Pocket the Spoils - Common
- If a reward is claimed this scene, draw 1.

18. Haggle - Common
- In a social scene, gain 1 reward draw or +2 on the bargain test.

19. Cinder Familiar - Elite
- Ally. HP 1.
- Exhaust: +1 to a magical or support action.

20. Grave Hound - Elite
- Ally. HP 2.
- Exhaust: +1 damage if another player already damaged that target this turn.

21. Tin Golem - Elite
- Ally. HP 3.
- Exhaust: prevent 1 damage to the party.

22. Lantern Sprite - Common
- Ally. HP 1.
- Exhaust: another player draws 1, then discards 1.

23. Dispel Draft - Common
- Deal 2 durability to an enchantment or ongoing curse.

24. Sharpen Edge - Common
- Ongoing.
- Your next damaging action gets +1 damage.

25. Prepared Ground - Elite
- Ongoing.
- The first time a hostile creature attacks this scene, prevent 1 damage.

Opening Hands Used In This Simulation
- Kael: Sharpen Edge, Shield Breaker, Quick Slash
- Brin: Treasure Nose, Hidden Stash, Pocket the Spoils
- Mira: Tin Golem, Study the Signs, Smoke Route
- Tamsin: Battle Prayer, Mercy Prayer, Cinder Familiar
