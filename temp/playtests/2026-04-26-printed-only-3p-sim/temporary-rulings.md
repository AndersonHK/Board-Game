# Temporary Rulings And Questions

These rulings were made only to keep the printed-materials-only simulation moving.

1. Setup dealing: starting hands were dealt one card at a time in fixed player order.
2. Prep scene: because no printed prep card exists, players treated escalation `0` as one non-hostile round that clears at end of round if nothing blocks it.
3. Shield persistence: shield tokens from `Warding Circle` and other non-Wizard effects persisted until removed. Only the Wizard active explicitly says its shields do not carry into the next scene.
4. Ongoing cards: resource cards with `Ongoing` or permanent placement were left on the field for ownership and Treasure scoring.
5. Exact named spawns: the table took the first available matching physical copy from the creature deck unless a matching card was already held aside as a scripted boss.
6. Criteria spawns: `Kennel Vault` used one top-to-bottom pass and took the first legal matches, including the first `Iron Gate`.
7. Enemy targeting: because enemies must target a legal friendly creature first but do not name which one, players chose the target.
8. Multiple `Prepared Ground`: separate copies stacked on the first hostile creature attack each scene. This was strong and may need explicit confirmation.
9. Threshold 5 tax: `Furnace Warden Rises` counted as the next scene after threshold 5, so the first encounter-reward extra draw in that scene was taxed.
10. `Field Bandage`: party HP was not allowed to exceed the current player-count starting HP of `18`, following the package's recommended production default.
11. Smoke-Flood reward: `return 1 card from the discard pile to hand` was awarded to the active player who cleared the venting test.
12. Shrine resolver: the table assigned the `Shrine of Echoes` reward to the last player who accepted the bargain, because no printed resolver procedure exists.
13. `Ash Miasma` claim: a player who removed it with `Dispel Draft` claimed it for Glory, because claiming says removing a hostile or blocking condition can claim the card.
14. `d3`: rolled `1d6`, reading `1-2` as `1`, `3-4` as `2`, and `5-6` as `3`.
15. Final ordeal extra turns: after the printed round structure completed, the table alternated one full player action turn for all players, then one enemy turn, with no standard draw.
16. Warrior active reset: the Warrior active was treated as once per player action turn, including final-ordeal extra player turns.
17. Magical targeting: dragons were not treated as magical for `Arc Bolt` unless their card explicitly said magical.
