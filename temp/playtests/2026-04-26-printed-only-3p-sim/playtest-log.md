# Playtest Log

## Setup

Aria chose Warrior, Bram chose Wizard, and Cora chose Cleric. Party HP began at `18`, escalation at `0`.

Aria received `First Name in the Ballad`, Bram received `Ashes to Ashes`, and Cora received `Richer Than the Ruin`.

The table used the printed board spaces for quest, party HP, escalation, encounter, decks, discard piles, shared party-controlled cards, and player score piles. Used markers were needed for once-per-scene memory, especially `Prepared Ground`, `Warding Circle`, `Greedfire Vow`, and threshold 5.

## Prep Scene, Escalation 0

The printed package says to begin with the quest's prep scene, but no prep card or prep-scene procedure was printed. The table ran one non-hostile round.

Cora played `Prepared Ground` and `Warding Circle`. Bram held resources. Aria had no meaningful target. Each player took the standard draw. At end of round, `Warding Circle` placed `1` shield on the party. The prep scene cleared and escalation rose to `1`.

## Scene 1: Cracked Causeway, Escalation 1

Reveal saves: Aria `5`, Bram `5`, Cora `6`; all succeeded.

Aria spent an action to secure the crossing. Because the encounter says `Spend 1 action to secure the crossing`, no test was rolled. The scene cleared immediately. The reward scouted `Soot-Stall Trader` on top of the encounter deck.

After the standard draw, Aria summoned `Grave Hound`, paying `1` party HP. End state: party HP `17`, escalation `2`, party shields `2`.

## Scene 2: Soot-Stall Trader, Escalation 2

Trader stock:

- Battle Prayer
- Mercy Prayer
- Summon Tin Golem
- Mercy Prayer
- Summon Cinder Familiar

Each player used a direct trade. Aria traded into `Summon Cinder Familiar`, Bram traded into `Mercy Prayer`, and Cora traded into `Summon Tin Golem`.

Cora summoned `Tin Golem`, discarding a card for the cost. Aria summoned `Cinder Familiar`. The scene cleared after all players traded or passed. End state: party HP `17`, escalation `3`, party shields `3`.

## Scene 3: Bound Pilgrim, Escalation 3

The scene spawned `Rusted Chains`.

Aria used `Blood Price Burst`, paying `1` party HP and dealing enough damage to remove the chains. She claimed `Rusted Chains`. The encounter reward healed `1` party HP, returning the party to `17`.

Aria later played `Hidden Stash`, drawing two cards, and then played `Warding Circle`. End state: party HP `17`, escalation `4`, party shields `4`.

## Scene 4: Smoke-Flood Gallery, Escalation 4

On reveal, Aria and Bram discarded cards. Cora chose party damage; a shield absorbed it.

Cora attempted the venting test and rolled `4`, succeeding by the default printed test number. The reward returned one card from the discard pile to Cora's hand. This required a ruling because the reward does not say whose hand receives the card.

Cora played `Greedfire Vow`, paying `1` party HP. The scene cleared. End state: party HP `16`, escalation `5`, party shields `4`.

Threshold 5 triggered after the scene ended, so `Furnace Warden Rises` was revealed next.

## Scripted Scene: Furnace Warden Rises, Escalation 5

The scene spawned `Ashen Warden`.

Action Turn 1:

- Bram played `Rally Push` to give Aria and Cora `+1` damage this turn.
- Cora played `Blood Price Burst`, paying `1` party HP and dealing `5` damage with the support bonus.
- Aria played `Arc Bolt` for the last `2` damage and claimed `Ashen Warden`.

Aria rolled `2` for Warrior Kill Reward against a Threat 3 Elite monster and drew a card.

The encounter reward made each player draw `1`. Because this was treated as the first scene after threshold 5, Aria took the threshold tax and discarded one card after the reward. Cora's `Greedfire Vow` also triggered when she gained an extra draw, drawing one more card and setting up a party HP loss at round end.

At round end, `Greedfire Vow` cost `1` party HP. End state: party HP `13`, escalation `6`, party shields `2`.

## Scene 5: Ember-Bell Nursery, Escalation 6

The scene spawned `2` `Ember Bat` cards and `1` `Ash Cultist`. `Ash Cultist` forced one player to discard; Cora took the discard.

Action Turn 1:

- Aria used Warrior active to defeat `Ash Cultist` and claimed it. Her Kill Reward roll was `3`, which failed for a Threat 1 Common monster.
- Bram defeated one `Ember Bat` with `Quick Slash`.
- Cora defeated the other `Ember Bat` with `Arc Bolt`.

The scene cleared in one round, so each player was allowed to keep one extra card at cleanup. End state: party HP `13`, escalation `7`, party shields `4`.

## Scene 6: Kennel Vault, Escalation 7

Using one top-to-bottom creature deck pass, the scene spawned:

- `Iron Gate`
- `Orc Raider`
- `Gray Wolf`
- `Gray Wolf`

Action Turn 1:

- Bram played `Shield Breaker` into `Iron Gate`, leaving it at `1` HP.
- Cora damaged a `Gray Wolf`.
- Aria used Warrior active against `Orc Raider`.

Enemy Turn 1:

- `Orc Raider` attacked first. Two `Prepared Ground` copies reduced the first hostile attack enough to prevent damage.
- Both `Gray Wolf` attacks hit `Tin Golem`. Their on-hit discard pressure exposed uncertainty around current claimant and support timing.

Action Turn 2:

- Cora used `Dispel Draft` to destroy `Iron Gate` and claimed it.
- Aria used `Last Step` to finish `Orc Raider`, claimed it, drew from `Last Step`, and failed the Warrior reward roll.
- Bram used `Cinder Familiar` to defeat the damaged `Gray Wolf`.

Enemy Turn 2:

- The last `Gray Wolf` defeated `Tin Golem`.

The scene was unresolved at round end. `Kennel Vault` made the current claimant discard, and escalation rose to `8`.

Round 2:

Aria used Warrior active to defeat the final `Gray Wolf`. Her Warrior reward roll was `6`, failing for a Threat 1 Common monster. The encounter reward gave Aria one extra resource draw, then threshold 5 forced her to discard one card. Aria played `Field Bandage` after the scene cleared, healing `2` party HP.

End state: party HP `15`, escalation `9`, party shields `8`.

## Scene 7: Shrine of Echoes, Escalation 9

Aria and Cora accepted the shrine bargain, each drawing and discarding. Because two players accepted, `Ash Miasma` spawned.

The table did not know who the `Resolver` was for the reward. Temporary ruling: Cora was the resolver because she was the last player to accept the bargain. Cora gained the reward draw, took the threshold 5 tax, and triggered her `Greedfire Vow`.

Aria used `Dispel Draft` to remove `Ash Miasma` and claimed it. The scene cleared. Bram played `Greedfire Vow` after the scene cleared.

At round end, Cora lost `1` party HP from `Greedfire Vow`. End state: party HP `14`, escalation `10`, party shields `10`.

Threshold 10 triggered after the scene ended, so `Dragon in the Deep` was revealed.

## Final Ordeal: Dragon in the Deep, Escalation 10

The scene spawned `Chainbound Head`.

Action Turn 1:

- Aria used Warrior active for `2` damage to `Chainbound Head`.
- Bram played `Quick Slash` for `1` damage.
- Cora played `Arc Bolt` for `1` damage. Dragons were not treated as magical for `Arc Bolt` because the printed dragon cards do not say `magical`.

Enemy Turn 1:

`Chainbound Head` attacked. Threshold 8 and two `Prepared Ground` copies both applied to the first hostile creature attack of the scene, reducing the damage to `1`. A friendly `Cinder Familiar` absorbed the hit and was defeated.

After the standard draw, Action Turn 2 began:

- Aria played `Shield Breaker`, discarding one card, and defeated `Chainbound Head`.
- `Heartfire Dragon` spawned.
- Bram sacrificed his `Cinder Familiar` to `Bonefuel Volley`, dealing `5` damage to `Heartfire Dragon`.
- Cora played `Quick Slash`, bringing `Heartfire Dragon` to `6` damage.

Enemy Turn 2:

`Heartfire Dragon` used `Dragon's Breath`. All three players chose party damage instead of discard. The d3 rolls were `1`, `2`, and `1`, removing `4` party shields. Then the dragon attacked and defeated `Grave Hound`.

The ordeal was still unresolved after Enemy Turn 2, so the final ordeal rule started extra alternating turns with no standard draw.

Extra Player Turn:

Aria used Warrior active for `2` damage, defeated `Heartfire Dragon`, and claimed it. The party won.
