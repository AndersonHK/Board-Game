Rulings Log For Quest 005

RUL-01 - Resource deck count conflict
Issue:
- The resource-deck README still says `64` cards, but the live card files total `72` copies.
Smallest ruling used:
- Build the session deck from the explicit card-file `Copies In Deck` values and treat the live deck as `72` cards.
Why this was minimal:
- It deferred to the more specific card data rather than inventing new counts.
Impact on the run:
- The session could use a real finite deck without silently deleting card copies.
Promotion target:
- `defines/cards/resource-deck/README.md`

Why this was minimal:

RUL-05 - Scene ending timing after an early clear
Issue:
- The quick start defines round order and also says a cleared scene ends, but it does not say whether players still take the remaining steps if the room is solved during Action Turn 1.
Smallest ruling used:
- Once a scene is fully cleared, the scene ends immediately and unused later steps are skipped.
Why this was minimal:
- It prevents free post-clear farming and matches plain-language reading of "the scene ends."
Impact on the run:
- Several scenes ended in Action Turn 1, which materially reduced card churn and clarified pacing.
Promotion target:
- quick start

RUL-07 - How hostile and blocking cards become scoreable claims
Issue:
- Prestige scoring and agendas both depend on players knowing which hostile or blocking cards they personally claimed for `Glory Points`, but the player-facing materials do not currently define that claim procedure.
Smallest ruling used:
- The player who deals the final damage or performs the final clearing action on a hostile or blocking card claims it for `Glory Points`.
Why this was minimal:
- It maps naturally onto existing kill-credit intuition.
Impact on the run:
- Kael and Brin had legible competing `Glory Points`, and endgame prestige could be calculated without a proxy system.
Promotion target:
- quick start or quest/scoring reference

RUL-08 - Current claimant tracking

Why this was minimal:
- It gave the phrase a concrete table meaning without adding a new widget.
Impact on the run:

Promotion target:
