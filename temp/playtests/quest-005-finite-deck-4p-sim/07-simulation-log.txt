Log note:
- This file is a narrative simulation summary, not a strict action-by-action ledger.
- It reflects an older timing assumption from before the current canon required a mandatory standard draw every round and full player/enemy turn participation if able.
- In longer unresolved scenes, routine actions were sometimes compressed instead of being logged one by one.

Starting state:
- Party HP: 11
- Escalation: 0
- Starting hands are the four hands listed in [02-player-roster.txt](./02-player-roster.txt)

Scene 0 - Sealed Stair Landing
- Mira spends her first action on `Prepared Ground`, turning the descent into a safer room before anyone sees a monster.
- Tamsin spends her first action on `Summon Cinder Familiar`, giving the party a live allied piece before the dungeon starts taxing hands.
- Those two prep actions satisfy the session prep-scene clear condition immediately.
- The scene ends before the normal mid-round draw because the run used an immediate-end-on-clear ruling.
- End state: party HP 11, escalation 1.

Scene 1 - Soot-Stall Trader
- Trader stock revealed from the top of the resource deck: `Warding Circle`, `Battle Prayer`, `Warding Circle`, `Prepared Ground`, `Chain Cutter`.
- Kael keeps `Dispel Draft` because he does not trust the ruin to stop making enchantments.
- Brin haggles high, trades away `Quick Slash`, and takes `Prepared Ground` plus `Chain Cutter`.
- Mira swaps out `Greedfire Vow` for `Warding Circle`; she wants durable value, not party-HP debt.
- Tamsin passes, preferring to keep both summons and `Last Step`.
- All four players used or passed their one trader interaction in Action Turn 1, so the scene ended immediately.
- End state: party HP 11, escalation 2.

Scene 2 - Cracked Causeway
- Save results:
  - Kael rolls 5 and passes.
  - Brin rolls 3; Tamsin spends her once-per-scene Cleric passive to make it 4, flipping the failure to success.
  - Because Brin would have lost a card, he chooses `Chain Cutter` as the saved card and puts it into Tamsin's hand instead of discarding it.
  - Mira rolls 2 and fails, discarding the `Warding Circle` she had just picked up from the trader.
  - Tamsin rolls 4 and passes.
- Tamsin immediately uses the rescued `Chain Cutter` on the crossing test, rolls safely above the session's default `4+` target, and secures the causeway in Action Turn 1.
- Mira uses the scene reward to scout the next encounter and sees `Ember-Bell Nursery`.
- End state: party HP 11, escalation 3.

Scene 3 - Ember-Bell Nursery
- The scene spawns `Ash Cultist`, `Ember Bat A`, and `Ember Bat B`.
- `Ash Cultist`'s reveal discard is aimed at Kael; he drops `Quick Slash` to keep `Shield Breaker` and `Dispel Draft`.
- Kael uses his Warrior active to kill `Ember Bat A`, but misses the Warrior reward roll.
- Brin uses his Warrior active to kill `Ash Cultist`, succeeds on the low-threshold Warrior roll, and draws `Field Bandage`.
- Mira uses `Arc Bolt` for full flying damage and removes `Ember Bat B`.
- The room is clear before Tamsin even needs to act.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Kael claims `Ember Bat A` (Treasure Value 1).
  - Brin claims `Ash Cultist` (Treasure Value 2).
  - Mira claims `Ember Bat B` (Treasure Value 1).
- End state: party HP 11, escalation 4.

Scene 4 - Shrine of Echoes
- Kael and Tamsin decline the bargain.
- Brin and Mira each take it once.
- Brin draws `Quick Slash`, decides it is not worth the slot, and discards it immediately.
- Mira draws `Lockpick Kit`, keeps it for a moment, then discards it because her hand is already tilted toward long-game value.
- Because two players took the bargain, `Ash Miasma` spawns.
- No one can or wants to spend the resources to dispel the miasma here, and the shrine's own clear condition is already satisfied.
- Brin takes the optional extra reward draw from the shrine and gets `Smoke Route`.
- The party leaves with a selfish win on paper and a hostile enchantment still hanging in the air.
- End state: party HP 11, escalation 5.

Scene 5 - Furnace Warden Rises
- `Ashen Warden` enters play.
- `Ash Miasma` from the shrine is treated as still affecting the room because the previous scene explicitly carried it to the next scene.

Round 1
- Mira uses her Wizard active immediately, placing 2 shields on the party.
- Kael spends his Warrior active for 2 damage on `Ashen Warden`.
- Brin uses `Prepared Ground` instead of pushing damage, making the table slightly safer but obviously also saving his own removal for later.
- Tamsin summons `Grave Hound`, accepting a slow setup line instead of emergency healing.
- The scene is not cleared, so the party gets its single default mid-encounter draw for this ordeal.
- On Turn 2, the players visibly sandbag the last few damage points instead of overcommitting, mostly because Kael and Brin both want the kill.
- Creature step:
  - `Ashen Warden` uses `Furnace Sweep`, removing Mira's 2 party shields.
  - It then attacks; `Prepared Ground` reduces that first hostile attack by 1, so the party only loses 1 HP.
- Round end:
  - The ordeal is unresolved, so escalation rises to 6.
  - `Ashen Warden` survives, so the party loses another 1 HP.
  - `Ash Miasma` forces the player with the most cards to discard; Mira throws away `Hidden Stash` rather than lose her stored burst cards.

Round 2
- Kael finally spends `Shield Breaker`, discarding a weaker card and dealing the final 3 damage to `Ashen Warden`.
- Because `Ashen Warden` is Threat 3 and Elite, Kael's Warrior draw is guaranteed.
- On clear, each player draws 1 card from the finite resource deck as printed on the ordeal card.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Kael claims `Ashen Warden` (Treasure Value 5).
- End state: party HP 9, escalation 7.

Scene 6 - Bound Pilgrim
- `Rusted Chains` enters as the blocking artifact.
- The miasma from earlier is present as lingering pressure, but the rescue scene itself only cares about removing `Rusted Chains` and any actually blocking carry-over card.
- Kael uses `Lockpick Kit` on the restraint test and clears `Rusted Chains` cleanly in Action Turn 1.
- The active rescue reward heals 1 party HP.
- Tamsin never needs to cash in her healing active here because the room clears too quickly.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Kael claims `Rusted Chains` as the cleared blocking hurdle (Treasure Value 1).
- End state: party HP 10, escalation 8.

Scene 7 - Reliquary of Cinders
- `Blessing Brazier` enters under party control.
- `Ash Miasma` enters as the hostile enchantment for this scene.
- Kael finally uses the `Dispel Draft` he refused to trade away at the merchant, removing the miasma outright.
- Brin spends his action to claim the reliquary reward once the room is safe enough to loot.
- Mira uses the downtime to put `Warding Circle` onto her field, continuing her slow-value line.
- Brin chooses to take the scene's extra reward draw anyway and gets `Blood Price Burst`, but because the quest's threshold-5 penalty is now live, he must discard 1 card immediately after the reward resolves.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Brin is treated as the claimant for the reliquary reward.
- End state: party HP 10, escalation 9.

Scene 8 - Kennel Vault
- Criteria spawn check against the session reserve produces `Smoke Seer`, `Gray Wolf`, `Ember Bat A`, and `Iron Gate`.
- Because escalation 8 is already live, the first hostile creature attack this scene would get +1 Attack if any hostile survives to attack.

Round 1
- Kael kills `Ember Bat A` with his Warrior active but again misses the low Warrior roll.
- Brin pays 1 party HP for `Blood Price Burst` and cleanly erases `Smoke Seer`, which is both efficient and very on-brand for his agenda.
- Mira banks value instead of chasing the remaining claim, adding another defensive permanent instead of racing Kael.
- Tamsin pings `Iron Gate` so she becomes the current claimant on the still-blocking hurdle.
- The encounter reaches the default mid-encounter draw because `Gray Wolf` and `Iron Gate` remain.
- On Turn 2, Tamsin spends her active to heal the party back up by 1, while Kael uses his Warrior active to kill `Gray Wolf`.
- No one finishes `Iron Gate`, partly because the table is short on exact gate text and partly because Kael wants the next round's cleaner claim line.
- Round end:
  - The scene remains unresolved, so escalation rises from 9 to 10.
  - Because `Iron Gate` is still the last blocking hurdle and Tamsin was the last player to progress it, she is treated as the current claimant for the encounter's discard penalty and loses 1 card.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Brin claims `Smoke Seer` (Treasure Value 3).
  - Kael claims `Ember Bat A` / vault copy (Treasure Value 1).
  - Kael claims `Gray Wolf` (Treasure Value 1).

Round 2
- The final ordeal is not revealed yet because the vault scene is still live.
- Brin uses `Battle Prayer` on Kael to give him +1 damage against the gate.
- Kael uses his Warrior active for 3 total damage and breaks `Iron Gate`.
- Because Kael cleared the last blocking hurdle, he gets the encounter's extra reward draw, then discards 1 card immediately due to the threshold-5 quest tax.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Kael claims `Iron Gate` (Treasure Value 2).
- The scene ends. Escalation is already 10, so the final ordeal now begins.

Scene 9 - Dragon in the Deep
- Stage 1 starts with `Chainbound Head`.
- When that stage falls, `Heartfire Dragon` enters.
- The escalation-10 no-turn-cap rule is now active.

Round 1
- Mira uses her Wizard active again for this new scene, placing 2 shields on the party.
- Kael and Brin split early damage onto `Chainbound Head` while Tamsin spends her turn preserving board presence instead of cashing out her hand.
- The party gets the single default mid-encounter draw for the final ordeal.
- In Action Turn 2, Brin delivers the final damage to `Chainbound Head` and claims it.
- `Heartfire Dragon` appears immediately after the head is destroyed and still takes the creature step that round.
- Kael lands the first point of damage on the dragon before creature step, but not enough to stop the counterattack.
- Creature step:
  - `Heartfire Dragon` uses `Dragon's Breath`. Two players discard and two rolls convert into party damage.
  - The dragon's first hostile attack of the scene is boosted by the escalation-8 quest threshold, but Mira's shields, `Prepared Ground`, and a live defensive summon absorb the entire hit.
- End state after round 1 of the ordeal: party HP 8, dragon wounded but still very live.

Round 2
- Brin spends more of his hand trying to stay ahead in hostile Treasure Value.
- Mira stops chasing kills and shifts fully into preservation, making sure the party can actually live long enough for the agenda math to matter.
- Tamsin finally uses her Cleric active to buy back 1 party HP instead of holding it forever.
- Kael and Brin push the dragon down to low HP, but Brin cannot quite line up the last hit before the creature step.
- `Dragon's Breath` fires again. This time the party loses enough cards and incidental HP that the finish suddenly matters more than score greed.
- `Warding Circle` and the surviving defensive pieces still keep the party alive, but only barely.
- End state after round 2 of the ordeal: party HP 5, Heartfire Dragon at 1 HP.

Round 3
- The table clearly sees the boss-kill race now.
- Brin has the better hostile-score pile, but Kael still has first crack at a clean finishing line.
- Kael uses his Warrior active for the last 2 damage needed, defeats `Heartfire Dragon`, and closes the game before another dragon turn happens.
- Because the dragon is Threat 3 Elite, Kael's Warrior reward draw is guaranteed, but by then the important part is the claim.
- Claimed pile changes:
  - Brin claims `Chainbound Head` (Treasure Value 6).
  - Kael claims `Heartfire Dragon` (Treasure Value 7).
