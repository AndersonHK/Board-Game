Simulation Roster: Four Players, Three Classes, One Quick Start Sheet

Seat order and draw order:
1. Kael Stonehand
2. Brin Ashvale
3. Mira Fen
4. Tamsin Vale

1. Kael Stonehand
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants the story to remember who finished the impossible fight
- Passive used: when he deals the final point of damage to a monster with his own card, creature, or Warrior active, roll 1d6 and draw 1 on `monster threat x rarity multiplier`
- Active used: once per turn, deal 2 damage
- Hand limit: 6
- Hidden priority: finish the dragon if the table leaves even a one-point opening

2. Brin Ashvale
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants the ruin to pay him in trophies, not gratitude
- Passive used: same Warrior kill-reward rule as Kael
- Active used: once per turn, deal 2 damage
- Hand limit: 6
- Hidden priority: stack claimed hostile Treasure Value high enough to make everyone else feel late

3. Mira Fen
- Class: Wizard
- Persona: wants to survive with more options than anyone else
- Passive used: hand limit 12
- Active used: once per scene, place 2 shield tokens on the party or any creature
- Hand limit: 12
- Hidden priority: finish the game rich in cards and still protected by a live engine

4. Tamsin Vale
- Class: Cleric
- Persona: wants to matter in every crisis even when that costs her the win
- Passive used: once per scene, when another player makes a save, give that roll +1; if that flips failure to success, the helped player chooses 1 card they would have lost and puts it into Tamsin's hand instead
- Active used: once per scene, heal 1 party HP or 2 HP to any creature
- Hand limit: 6
- Hidden priority: prove the table needed her, even if her actual agenda ends up harder than it looks

Starting-Hand Summary:
- Kael: `Shield Breaker`, `Dispel Draft`, `Quick Slash`
- Brin: `Shield Breaker`, `Rally Push`, `Quick Slash`
- Mira: `Arc Bolt`, `Greedfire Vow`, `Prepared Ground`
