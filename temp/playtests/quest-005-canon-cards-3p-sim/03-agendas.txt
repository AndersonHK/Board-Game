Secret Agendas Used For This Run

Important note:
- Unlike Quest 004, this run uses the actual canonical agenda cards instead of temporary tighter-threshold prototypes.
- The session still needs a ruling for how hostile-card claiming works, because the visible materials do not yet teach that procedure clearly enough.

Assignments

1. Rowan Flintbrand -> First Name in the Ballad
- Goal Text: succeed if you defeat Heartfire Dragon or finish with the highest total Treasure Value from hostile cards you claimed
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

2. Elira Ashglass -> Prepared Beyond Reason
- Goal Text: succeed if you end the game with at least 12 Treasure Value in hand and on-field owned cards
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

3. Sister Maelin -> By My Grace
- Goal Text: succeed if you cause 3 shared-success moments or 2 failed saves to become successes
- Reward Text: gain 20 prestige points

Why This Mix Was Chosen
- Rowan's agenda pressures last hits and boss credit.
- Elira's agenda pressures draw, retention, and safe storage.
- Maelin's agenda pressures exactly the rescue-and-profit behavior the Cleric class is meant to create.
