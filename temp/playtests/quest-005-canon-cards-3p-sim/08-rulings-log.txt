Rulings Log For Quest 005

RUL-01 - Vertical-slice build size versus full-deck size
Issue:
- During the run, the repo looked like it was contradicting itself on encounter and action-card counts.
Smallest ruling used:
- Treat `8` random encounters and `64` action cards as vertical-slice printable minimums, not final gameplay caps, and build one legal session-sized subset from the larger libraries.
Why this was minimal:
- It preserves the playable session build without forcing the full game's future deck sizes to stay tiny.
Impact on the run:
- The session remained reproducible, and the later user clarification confirmed this was the intended interpretation.
Promotion target:
- already clarified in docs

RUL-02 - Action order inside each action turn
Issue:
- The quick start says each player takes 1 action per turn, but it does not say whether order is fixed, rotating, or chosen.
Smallest ruling used:
- The table chooses action order each turn.
Why this was minimal:
- It adds no new widget and lets support timing matter.
Impact on the run:
- Maelin's Battle Prayer timing became strategically meaningful in several scenes.
Promotion target:
- quick start

RUL-03 - Default target number for non-combat tests
Issue:
- Several encounter cards and utility cards refer to non-combat tests, but the visible player-facing rules only define a default number for saves.
Smallest ruling used:
- If a non-combat test has no printed target number, it succeeds on `4+` on a `d6`, matching the save baseline.
Why this was minimal:
- It reuses the only explicit default number the quick start currently teaches.
Impact on the run:
- Cracked Causeway and Smoke-Flood Gallery were playable without inventing a second dice system.
Promotion target:
- quick start

RUL-04 - Named spawns versus criteria-spawn order
Issue:
- The creature-deck rules explain criteria-based searches, but the visible materials still do not explain how exact-name searches should interact with that ordered reserve.
Smallest ruling used:
- Exact-name spawns search the reserve for the named card without changing the session's criteria-spawn order.
Why this was minimal:
- It preserves the creature-deck grammar without making named spawns accidentally reshuffle future criteria scenes.
Impact on the run:
- Kennel Break and Dragon in the Deep stayed easy to execute, and Kennel Vault still had a stable criteria result later.
Promotion target:
- design doc or creature-deck define

RUL-05 - Claimed hostile cards and the personal score pile
Issue:
- The agenda cards and prestige scoring now imply a claimed hostile-card economy, but the quick start still does not explain who claims defeated hostile cards.
Smallest ruling used:
- The player who deals the final point of damage to a hostile card claims it into a personal score pile.
Why this was minimal:
- It aligns with the most intuitive "credit for the final hit" reading.
Impact on the run:
- The actual agenda cards became usable, especially First Name in the Ballad.
Promotion target:
- quick start or core-rules prestige section

RUL-06 - The meaning of `current claimant`
Issue:
- Gray Wolf and Kennel Vault refer to the `current claimant`, but the visible materials never define that term.
Smallest ruling used:
- The current claimant is the player currently closest to the scene reward, defined here as the player who most recently advanced the last remaining hostile or blocking hurdle.
Why this was minimal:
- It turns the phrase into a visible state derived from the scene instead of adding a new token.
Impact on the run:
- Kennel Vault's unresolved-round discard could resolve without a table argument.
Promotion target:
- quick start or encounter glossary

RUL-07 - Threshold-5 reward-draw timing
Issue:
- The quest says the first player each scene to gain extra reward draws must discard 1 after that reward resolves, but some reward draws can happen in multi-player contexts.
Smallest ruling used:
- The player who triggered or cleared the reward counts as the first player to gain that extra reward draw.
Why this was minimal:
- It anchors the rule to a visible cause rather than trying to invent simultaneous priority.
Impact on the run:
- Kennel Break and continued Kennel Vault both resolved cleanly.
Promotion target:
- quest card or quick start

RUL-08 - Summon and enchantment scoring
Issue:
- Resource cards such as Summon Tin Golem and Warding Circle create matching entity cards, but the quick-start scoring text would double-count them unless the table knows how to treat that transformation.
Smallest ruling used:
- Score the resulting permanent once rather than both the spent spell card and the permanent it created.
Why this was minimal:
- It matches how a real player would intuitively think about "the thing I own on the field."
Impact on the run:
- Endgame prestige was tallied without obvious double counting.
Promotion target:
- quick start prestige example
