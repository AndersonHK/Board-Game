Simulation Roster: Three Players, Three Unique Classes

1. Rowan Flintbrand
- Class: Warrior
- Persona: wants the story to remember who ended the biggest threats
- Quick-start passive: when Rowan deals the final point of damage to a monster with his own action card, creature, or Warrior trait damage, roll 1d6 and draw 1 if the result is less than or equal to `monster threat x rarity multiplier`
- Quick-start active: once per turn, Rowan may deal `2` damage
- Hidden priority: boss credit, hostile-card claims, and visible kills

2. Elira Ashglass
- Class: Wizard
- Persona: wants to leave the ruin with the most options and the calmest hands
- Quick-start passive: hand limit `12`
- Quick-start active: once per scene, place `2` shield tokens on the party or any creature
- Hidden priority: end the game loaded with Treasure Value and enough retained cards to validate the Wizard plan

3. Sister Maelin
- Class: Cleric
- Persona: wants every rescue to matter and every ally to remember the rescue
- Quick-start passive: once per scene, when another player makes a save, give that roll `+1`; if that flips failure into success, that player chooses `1` card they would have lost and puts it into Maelin's hand instead
- Quick-start active: once per scene, heal `1` party HP or `2` HP to any creature
- Hidden priority: create enough shared-success moments that her help is both useful and profitable

Starting Hand Notes:
- All players use the quest card's three-player starting hand size of `4`.
- Rowan and Maelin use the baseline hand limit of `6`.
- Elira uses the Wizard hand limit of `12` from the start of the game.
- This run keeps class selection unique because three-player mode no longer needs the four-player duplicate-class patch.
